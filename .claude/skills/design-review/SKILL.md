---
name: design-review
description: Review the website against its brief for visual quality, generic design patterns, usability, and responsive issues. Use when asked to critique or polish the page.
---

Read CLAUDE.md and the files under brief/.
Inspect relevant references and supplied photographs.

Review the rendered website using available browser tools.
Check approximately 390px, 768px, and 1440px viewport widths.
If browser tools are unavailable, state that the review is code-only.

Evaluate:
- Whether the page feels specific to this person or business.
- Whether the main offer and next action are immediately clear.
- Typography, line lengths, spacing, contrast, and hierarchy.
- Photo selection, cropping, and relationship to the palette.
- Repetitive layouts, unnecessary containers, and decorative clutter.
- Vague copy, invented claims, and missing content.
- Mobile overflow, navigation, keyboard focus, and button behavior.

Report the five most important issues, highest impact first.
For each, identify the location, explain the problem, and propose
a concrete correction grounded in the brief.

Do not redesign or edit unless asked.
Preserve approved choices.
Do not claim to have inspected screenshots you have not seen.
