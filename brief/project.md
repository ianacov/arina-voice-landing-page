# Project Brief

## Project overview

Create a single-page, Russian-language website for Arina, a teacher
working with voice and self-expression who offers an original course
and other ways to study with her.

The first version should establish a credible professional presence,
introduce her teaching approach, explain her offers, and help visitors
take the next step toward studying with her.

The website should be suitable for sharing through social media and
directly with prospective students.

## Business context

Arina is currently recruiting the second cohort of her course.

Her Telegram channel already contains substantial information about
her work, including pinned posts describing ways to work with her
and available pricing options.

The website should organize the relevant information into a clear,
coherent introduction. It should not reproduce the entire channel.

The immediate priority is a complete, publishable first version.
Content and photographs can be updated after launch.

## Primary goal

Help prospective students understand Arina's work, assess whether
an offer suits them, and find a clear way to enquire or enrol.

The current course intake should be easy to find.

## Supporting goals

- Present Arina as a teacher with a recognizable personal identity.
- Build trust through real photographs and specific information.
- Explain the course and other confirmed ways to work with her.
- Provide one consistent link she can share with potential students.
- Make future course announcements and photo updates straightforward.

## Audience

Primary audience:
Russian-speaking prospective students interested in voice and
self-expression.

Expected entry points:
- Arina's social media and Telegram channel.
- Links shared directly with prospective students.
- Personal recommendations.

The audience's specific goals, experience levels, and suitability
criteria still need to be clarified.

Do not assume an age range, gender, profession, or prior training.

## Visitor questions

The page should help a visitor answer:

1. Who is Arina, and what does she teach?
2. Is her approach relevant to my needs?
3. What can I study with her?
4. What does the course involve?
5. Is enrolment currently open?
6. Where can I see practical details and pricing?
7. How do I ask a question or join?

## First-version scope

Confirmed scope:
- One public-facing page.
- An introduction to Arina and her work.
- Information about her original course.
- Information about other confirmed ways to study with her.
- A selection of supplied photographs.
- A clear contact or enrolment route.
- Responsive presentation for mobile and desktop.

Proposed content structure:
1. Opening introduction and primary action.
2. About Arina and her teaching approach.
3. Course overview and current intake.
4. Other ways to work with her.
5. Relevant background and qualifications, once supplied.
6. Contact and next steps.

This structure is a starting point. Combine or reorder sections
according to the available content and visitor needs.

Do not create empty sections simply to follow this outline.

## Primary action

Recommended priority while enrolment is open:
Direct visitors toward the current course intake.

The exact action and destination must be confirmed:
- Contact Arina through Telegram.
- Complete an application form.
- Use another existing enrolment process.

Do not assume that a Telegram channel link is an enrolment link.
Do not create a form without an agreed submission destination.

## Course intake and updates

Confirmed:
- The upcoming intake is the second cohort.
- Arina wants to run additional cohorts.
- A regular schedule has not yet been established.

The discussion mentions 23 November, but the wording is ambiguous.
Confirm the exact date, year, and whether this is the course start
or an enrolment deadline before publishing it.

Do not advertise a monthly or every-other-month schedule as settled.

Keep permanent course information separate from changing intake
details such as dates, enrolment status, and current pricing.

Recommended future states:
- Enrolment open: show confirmed intake details and the joining action.
- Enrolment closed: show course information and a confirmed enquiry
  or future-intake notification option.

Only offer a waiting list if a real collection process exists.

## Visual objectives

The client wants a harmonious, welcoming page that inspires trust.

She expressed a preference for light, natural, pastel shades,
including pink and lilac.

Use the selected photographs to guide the final palette.
The page and photographs should feel visually consistent.

Choose photographs that present Arina primarily as a teacher.
Avoid selecting images solely for their theatrical or actress-like
character.

Detailed typography, colors, layout, and visual references belong
in brief/design.md.

## Content sources

Use:
- Client-supplied texts.
- Relevant Telegram posts, once provided or accessed.
- Confirmed course and pricing information.
- Client-supplied photographs.
- Verified background and qualifications.

The Telegram content has been described but has not yet been
incorporated into this brief.

Edit source material for web readability while preserving Arina's
meaning and personal voice.

Do not invent testimonials, credentials, student numbers, outcomes,
scarcity, or claims about the effectiveness of the course.

## Information needed before publication

- Public name and preferred professional title.
- Telegram channel and direct contact links.
- Short introduction and teaching approach.
- Course name, intended audience, and content.
- Format, location or platform, duration, and session schedule.
- Confirmed intake date and enrolment deadline, if applicable.
- Prices and what each option includes.
- Other available teaching formats.
- Relevant education and experience.
- Final photo selection.
- Primary action and its destination.

During development, track unresolved items explicitly.
Do not publish placeholders as established facts.

## Outside the initial scope

Unless separately requested:
- Student accounts or a learning platform.
- Online payments.
- Automated booking.
- A blog or Telegram feed integration.
- Multiple language versions.
- A custom administration dashboard.

## Completion criteria

The first version is ready when:
- Visitors can identify Arina's work and main offer quickly.
- Course details are accurate and the intake status is clear.
- Every contact or enrolment action works.
- The page reads naturally in Russian.
- Photographs and styling form a coherent visual presentation.
- Mobile and desktop layouts have been reviewed.
- Basic accessibility is checked.
- No invented claims or unresolved placeholders remain.
- Future intake details and photographs can be updated easily.
